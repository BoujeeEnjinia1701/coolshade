# CoolShade user requirements

What the people who wait, sell, run and fund a public cooling shade need, written from their side. The fifteen needs come from the users and stakeholders in CSH-PRB-001 Table 1 (transit riders, market traders and shoppers, people in queues, outdoor workers, the city, transit or market operator, community groups and schools) and from the first site decided on 2026-10-02, a market lane in a hot, dry city (CSH-DEC-001). They are used to score the concept options (CSH-OPT-001) and each one has at least one product requirement in CSH-PRS-001 (links in CSH-RTM-001). They remain a desk draft: no co-design session has been held and no partner has agreed to anything. Method: STANDARDS section 22.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Shade from the sun | I can stand out of direct sun, with the heat from the sun and hot surfaces cut, while I wait | High | Transit riders, queues, outdoor workers (PRB Table 1) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Cooler air | The air where I stand feels cooler than the street, also when it is still | High | Transit riders, market traders |
| UR-003 | Usability | I stay dry | My skin, clothes and goods do not get wet, and the ground does not get slippery | High | Transit riders, market traders |
| UR-004 | Usability | Room for everyone | There is room for a group to stand or sit, and for a wheelchair, and I do not hit my head | High | Queues, older adults, wheelchair users (PRB) |
| UR-005 | Performance | Works only when needed | It cools when it is hot and someone is there, and stops otherwise, so water and power are not wasted | Medium | Operator |
| UR-006 | Deployment and assembly | No power connection | It works where there is no mains power | High | Operator, market lane (PRB constraints) |
| UR-007 | Safety | Safe water | The mist and the water behind it cannot make me ill | High | All users; city and market operator |
| UR-008 | Safety | Stands up to wind and weather | It does not fall, fly or collapse in a gust or a storm | High | Operator, everyone nearby |
| UR-009 | Safety | Safe electrics | I cannot get a shock or start a fire, and nothing is hot or sharp where I can reach | High | All users, operator |
| UR-010 | Safety | Privacy | It does not watch, film or record me | Medium | Transit riders, market traders |
| UR-011 | Maintenance | Easy to keep running | Refilling, cleaning and clearing the nozzles take little time and few tools | Medium | Operator, community groups |
| UR-012 | Deployment and assembly | The community can build it | A community workshop or school can build it from common parts and tools | Medium | Community groups and schools |
| UR-013 | Logistics | It can be moved | Two people can move it to another site | Medium | Operator |
| UR-014 | Cost | Affordable | It costs no more than a market or a district can raise for one site | Medium | Operator, NGO |
| UR-015 | Environment and durability | Uses little water | A tank lasts at least a day and a design day uses little water | Medium | Operator, city water authority |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
